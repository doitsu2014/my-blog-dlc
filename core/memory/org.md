# Organisation Memory

Standing editorial rules that apply to every blog and publication this
organisation (or this author) runs. Edit this file to change behaviour across
all intents and spaces.

## Editorial Standards

- A human reads and approves every post before it is published.
- Every post has one named reader and one key takeaway.
- Every factual claim is sourced, softened to opinion, or removed.
- No fabricated quotes, anecdotes, statistics, testimonials, or citations.
- Quotes are verbatim and attributed; borrowed ideas are credited.

## Integrity and Disclosure

- Disclose sponsorships, affiliate links, and conflicts of interest.
- Disclose substantial AI assistance where the publication's policy asks.
- Corrections that change a conclusion are marked at the top of the post.

## Rights and Privacy

- Never publish an image, diagram, or code whose licence is unknown.
- No secrets, credentials, internal hostnames, or personal data in examples.
- No private information about real people without their consent.

## Accessibility

- Every image has meaningful alt text.
- Link text makes sense out of context.
- Headings form a real hierarchy; one H1 per post.
