# Phase 2 — Outline

Outline decides what the post argues and in what order, before a single
paragraph is drafted. Structural problems are cheap to fix in an outline and
expensive to fix in a draft, so this phase does the architecture work.

## Focus

Angle and structure.

## The AI's role

Thinking partner. The model proposes angles, alternative structures, and
headline variants, and pressure-tests them against the reader. The human
chooses the angle and owns the argument.

## Outputs

- An angle statement: thesis, promise to the reader, and why this take
- A section-by-section outline with the purpose of each section
- Headline options and the chosen headline
- The opening hook

## Gates

Every stage in Outline ends at a human approval gate.

## Exit criteria

- The thesis fits in one sentence and the human agrees with it
- Every section earns its place against the key takeaway
- The headline makes a promise the outline keeps
- The hook states why the reader should keep reading

## Next phase

When Outline is approved, [Draft](../draft/phase.md) writes the post and its
supporting code and visuals.
