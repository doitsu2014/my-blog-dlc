# Phase 6 — Evolve

Evolve closes the loop. A published post is an asset: it can be measured,
corrected, refreshed, and reused. This phase usually runs days or weeks after
publication, so park the workflow after Publish and resume when there is data.

## Focus

Measurement and improvement.

## The AI's role

Content analyst. The model reads the analytics the human provides, compares
them with the goal in the content brief, and proposes refreshes and
derivatives. The human decides what is worth more effort.

## Outputs

- A performance report measured against the brief's success criteria
- A refresh plan for posts that are outdated, wrong, or underperforming
- Repurposed content for other formats and channels

## Gates

Every stage in Evolve ends at a human approval gate.

## Exit criteria

- Performance is judged against the brief, not against vanity metrics
- Every refresh item names what changes and why
- Lessons that should change future posts are proposed as learnings

## Next

A refresh plan becomes a new intent under the `refresh` profile. A
repurposing plan can start a new intent under the `repurpose` profile.
