# repurpose scope

`repurpose` turns an existing post into derivatives: a newsletter issue, a
thread, a LinkedIn post, a talk or deck outline, a video script, or a
cross-post on another platform.

## Why these stages

Brief Capture names the source post and the target formats. Audience
Analysis describes the reader on each new channel, who is often not the blog
reader. Repurposing adapts the verified source to each format and links back
to the original. No new facts are introduced, so research and editing are
skipped.

## Membership

Discover: brief-capture, audience-analysis.
Evolve: repurposing.

Everything else is SKIP.
