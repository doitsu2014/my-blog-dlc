# deep-dive scope

`deep-dive` is for long-form explainers, comparisons, benchmarks, and
investigations: posts whose value is the depth of their evidence. It runs
the full pre-publication lifecycle with a stricter guard policy and a larger
question budget.

Guard Policy is strict: when an approved research artifact changes, later
approvals that consumed it are reopened.

## Why these stages

Topic Research is expected to run (it self-skips only with a recorded
reason). The angle is stress-tested against its strongest counter-argument.
The developmental edit, technical review, and fact check apply the blocking
review cap: a wrong claim or an unsupported comparison does not ship.

Evolve is a separate intent, typically resumed weeks later.

## Membership

Discover, Outline, Draft, Refine, Publish: all stages.

Evolve is SKIP.
