# opinion scope

`opinion` is for essays, reflections, retrospectives, and arguments drawn
from the author's own experience. The value is the author's perspective, so
the profile spends its effort on the angle and the developmental edit, not
on research or search.

## Why these stages

Brief Capture and Audience Analysis define who the argument is for. Angle
Definition is the most important stage: the thesis must be specific enough
to disagree with, and its strongest counter-argument must be named. First
Draft leaves `[AUTHOR]` prompts for every personal story rather than
inventing one. Fact Check still runs — opinions rest on facts too. Publish
packages the post and plans distribution.

## Membership

Discover: brief-capture, audience-analysis.
Outline: all three stages.
Draft: first-draft.
Refine: developmental-edit, fact-check, copy-edit.
Publish: all three stages.

Everything else is SKIP.
